Common questions

How do we get a marriage license in [Your State]?

Apply at your county clerk's office. Both parties appear in person with valid ID. I walk clients through the timing and any waiting period before the wedding date.

Do you perform bilingual ceremonies?

Yes. Fully in English, fully in Spanish, or a mix. We decide which sections are translated based on who is in the room.

Can we write our own vows?

Yes, and most couples do. I provide prompts if you want them and leave you alone if you don't.

What is included?

An initial consultation, a written ceremony draft, revisions, a rehearsal if you want one, and the ceremony itself. Fees are quoted per wedding, not per hour.

How far in advance should we book?

Three to six months is typical. Shorter notice is often possible, especially for weekday ceremonies.
